Career Coaching

One-on-one sessions for researchers and scientists ready to take the next step

Who Is This For?

The Agritech and life sciences sector is specific — and it can be hard to see all the possibilities, inside or outside academia. After years working on a very specialised topic, it's not always clear where your skills can take you. That's exactly what we untangle together.

This service is designed for researchers, PhD students, postdocs, and scientists working in Agritech, environmental biology, biotech, and related fields — including people navigating international job markets and those who need to build or adapt their CV for a new audience.

What We Work On

Discover Your Direction

Find out what you're genuinely good at, what drains you, and what kind of work actually fits who you are.

Target the Right Opportunities

Map out job roles that match your profile — inside and outside academia, in industry, startups, regulatory bodies, and beyond.

Build Your Strategy

Tailor your CV for the right roles, craft your story, and define a concrete networking and application plan.

How It Works

No packages. No long-term commitment. A single focused session where we go deep on your situation and leave with a clear, actionable plan. Each session is tailored to you — your background, your goals, your constraints.

Sessions are conducted in English or Italian, online or in person in Barcelona.
