Step 04

Your CV Strategy

Speaking the right language to the right audience

1
Lead with impact, not tasks Show what changed because of your work — not just what you did.
2
Translate scientific language for industry Hiring managers outside academia don't speak your lab's vocabulary. Simplify ruthlessly.
3
Tailor every application One CV does not fit all. Each role deserves a version written for that specific context.
4
Academic vs. industry CV — know the difference Length, structure, and emphasis are completely different. Know which one to send.
Step 05

Networking & Application Strategy

How to find, reach, and land the right role

1
Build visibility before you need it The best time to start networking is before you're looking for a job.
2
LinkedIn: your scientific story, told simply Optimise your profile so the right people find you — not just the other way around.
3
Warm outreach beats cold applications A personal message to the right person is worth ten applications through a portal.
4
Track, iterate, and follow up Job searching is a process, not a single event. Keep records and refine as you go.
