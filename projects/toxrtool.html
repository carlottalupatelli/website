ToxRTool

Independent project · 2026

Overview

ToxRTool (Toxicological data Reliability Assessment Tool) is a scoring instrument developed by EURL ECVAM at the European Commission Joint Research Centre for judging the reliability of toxicological and ecotoxicological studies, based on the Klimisch criteria used throughout EU chemical and pesticide regulation. I built an open-source web implementation of it, approved by the original authors and the JRC, to make the assessment faster to run and easier to document.

A reviewer works through 21 criteria grouped into categories — test substance identification, test organism/species characterisation, study design, and results reporting — scoring each one and citing supporting text from the study under evaluation. The tool computes the total score and assigns the corresponding reliability category (Klimisch 1–4), and produces a shareable, exportable record of the assessment.

Latest update: AI-assisted draft assessment

The most recent update adds an AI draft step to the workflow. A reviewer uploads the study PDF, and the tool proposes a score and reasoning for each of the 21 criteria automatically — each answer backed by a citation that links directly to the exact page and section of the source document it was drawn from, so it can be checked against the original text in one click.

Nothing is applied to the assessment until the reviewer checks the draft: each item can be accepted, edited, or discarded individually before being applied. This turns a task that previously meant reading the full study and manually working through 21 criteria into a review-and-confirm pass, while keeping a human decision on every scored item.

Walkthrough of the AI draft assessment: uploading a study PDF, reviewing the proposed score and cited reasoning for each of the 21 criteria, and the resulting Klimisch reliability category.

Key features

  • Full implementation of the 21 official ToxRTool criteria, organised by category, with mandatory-criterion handling
  • AI draft assessment from an uploaded study PDF, with per-criterion reasoning and page/section-linked citations
  • Reviewer approval step — every AI-proposed answer can be accepted, edited, or discarded before being applied
  • Automatic scoring and Klimisch reliability category (1–4) assignment
  • Export and sharing via PDF, Excel, and shareable link
